### Background

Sarah has been in product operations for eight years. She started as a project coordinator and worked her way up by consistently delivering cross-functional projects on time. She manages a team of five and is the bridge between engineering, design, and business stakeholders. Her days are packed with standups, reviews, and async communication across Slack, Notion, and Jira.

---

### Goals

- Reduce time spent on repetitive reporting and status updates
- Improve visibility into team capacity and project health
- Make data-driven decisions without needing to dig through spreadsheets
- Onboard new team members faster with less hand-holding
- Ship high-quality features predictably and on schedule

---

### Pain Points

- **Information overload** — Too many tools, too many notifications, no single source of truth
- **Manual work** — Still copy-pasting data between tools for weekly reports
- **Unclear priorities** — Stakeholders frequently shift priorities without acknowledging trade-offs
- **Poor handoffs** — Specs get lost between teams; context disappears after meetings
- **Slow feedback loops** — Doesn't learn what customers actually think until after release

---

### Behaviors

- Checks dashboards first thing in the morning before email
- Prefers async communication but switches to calls when things are urgent
- Builds templates and checklists for every recurring process
- Advocates for the product internally; acts as a customer champion
- Reads industry blogs and newsletters to stay ahead (ProductLed, Lenny's Newsletter)

### Scenario: A Day with the Product

Sarah opens the product dashboard at 8:45 AM. Within two minutes she can see which projects are at risk, who's blocked, and what shipped yesterday — all without opening a single spreadsheet. She uses the AI summary feature to draft a stakeholder update in under five minutes instead of thirty. By 9:15 AM she's already in her first meeting, fully prepared.

---

### Design Implications

| Insight                              | Design Action                                        |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Prefers dashboards over reports      | Lead with visual summaries, not tables               |
| Time-poor; values speed              | Optimize for fewer clicks, smart defaults            |
| Manages across tools                 | Provide integrations with Jira, Slack, Notion        |
| Learns from peers                    | Build in-app case studies and team benchmarks        |
| Skeptical of new tools               | Offer guided onboarding and time-to-value metrics    |

---

### Anti-patterns to Avoid

- **Don't** require lengthy setup before showing value
- **Don't** bury key metrics behind multiple navigation layers
- **Don't** send generic notifications — make alerts actionable and specific
- **Don't** assume she has time to read long tooltips or documentation
